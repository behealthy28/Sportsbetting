Dixon-Coles xG Model
src/models/dixon_coles.py · Understat xG scraper
MLE-fitted Poisson goal model. Uses expected goals (xG) over actual goals — removes finishing luck. Scores a probability matrix over all (home goals × away goals) scorelines, sums to home/draw/away. Low-score correction (Dixon-Coles ρ) adjusts the 0-0, 1-0, 0-1, 1-1 cells.

Statistical Blend

Edge Calculation + Kelly Sizing
src/market/edge.py · src/market/kelly.py
Polymarket CLOB + Kalshi APIs polled for live market odds. Bookmaker margin devigged. Model probability minus devigged market probability = edge. Quarter-Kelly for conservative bankroll management.
